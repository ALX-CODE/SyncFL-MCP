# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. People have different levels of programming and music-production experience; help them reproduce problems without ridicule.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, doxxing and deliberately sharing private or unlicensed material are not welcome. Critique code and ideas rather than people. Keep support discussions relevant and avoid repeated unsolicited promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Report concerns privately to a listed maintainer when possible. Do not post someone else's personal information as evidence in a public issue.
